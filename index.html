Our story

Why “Lawnquistadors”?

Lawnquistadors started with two things that came together: a love of history and a desire to build something of my own.

I’ve always been interested in history, so when I was coming up with a name, the Spanish conquistador theme stood out to me. The idea of conquering challenges and pushing forward became the inspiration for Lawnquistadors.

I bring that same mindset to every lawn I work on. My goal is simple: do a good job, pay attention to the details, and leave your yard looking clean and well maintained.

Lawnquistadors is here to provide dependable lawn services to homeowners in Lincoln, Nebraska.

Levi, owner
